Dr. Peter Cotton is Chief Technology Officer of Global Strategic Minerals Corporation and the author of Microprediction: Building an Open AI Network, which anticipated the rise of decentralized artificial intelligence, agents, and prediction markets.

His theoretical contributions include a mathematical unification of the hierarchical and optimization-based branches of portfolio theory. His numerical contest inversion algorithms made it possible to scale econometric tools — the workhorse probit among them — to millions of variables. He also created Laplace, a state-of-the-art autonomous time-series algorithm.

In industry he has twice led data science teams at buy-side firms managing more than $10 billion. On the sell side, he pioneered the application of control theory to over-the-counter trading at JPMorgan and earlier led CDO model development at Morgan Stanley.

Among the companies he has co-founded is Benchmark Solutions, whose technology was integrated into Bloomberg. His open-source Python packages for time-series analysis, optimization and portfolio construction have together passed a million downloads. He holds a doctorate in mathematics from Stanford University.
